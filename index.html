About

A career built on paying attention.

  • Vertafore2015 – 2016

I'm a design leader who thrives at the intersection of systems, story, and human behavior. I bring clarity to complex ecosystems, shaping products, platforms, and design systems that scale with intention. My work spans startups and enterprise organizations, translating ambiguity into aligned, user-centered experiences that empower both teams and customers.

I've designed in the age of AI, including an AI tool I conceived during a hackathon that was later refined and taken to production by another designer on my team. I use AI in my process to prototype faster and ask sharper questions, while thinking carefully about what it means to build experiences that are intuitive, trustworthy, and genuinely human-centered. Read more about how I design →

I also spent time on onboarding during Compass's acquisition of Anywhere, when the agent base grew roughly 10x almost overnight. It's a narrower story than the rest of my work here, but usability testing surfaced a finding about trust in AI assistance that's stuck with me. Read that story →

Rooted in visual craft and driven by deep observation, I build with dedication, collaborate without ego, and believe the most impactful design emerges from understanding people first. Outside of in-house work, I run Soak Design, an independent practice, and I'm currently open to Senior or Lead Product Designer roles at product companies with a real premise, as well as fractional and consulting engagements. If you're building something meaningful and want design that is both thoughtful and resilient, I'd love to connect.
